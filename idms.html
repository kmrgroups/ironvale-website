Sample data

Fills the system with one worked example — a customer, a part, a priced customer link, a four-operation routing and its dimensions — so the whole chain can be seen end to end. Everything it creates is marked, and Remove sample data deletes exactly those records and nothing else.

Add a part

A part is created automatically when a quotation is won on the website. Add one by hand only for work that did not come through that route.
Issued by the database — never typed.
A part is yours, not a customer's. Customers, their own part numbers and their prices are added to the part once it exists — one part can go to several customers at different prices.

Customers for this part

The same part can go to several customers, each with their own part number and their own price. Adding a customer here does not change the part itself.

Goods Receipt Note

The GRN number is issued by the database when you save, using this company's document prefix. Two people saving at the same moment cannot be given the same number.

Received

Delivery Challan

Material going out to a customer. The challan number is issued by the database when you save. Add lines before saving — one challan can carry several parts.
Lines

Despatched

Process FMEA

Drafted by AI from the routing and the dimensions already on the part — every line is tied to a real operation and a real characteristic. Severity, occurrence and detection are starting points for your CFT to argue with, not answers. Nothing here is approved until a named team signs it.

PFMEA

Cross-functional team sign-off

A PFMEA is a team judgement, not a document one person produces. Record who reviewed it — the printed copy shows this, and until it is filled the print is stamped DRAFT.

Process Flow Diagram

Drawn from the routing — it is not typed separately, so it can never disagree with the operations you entered. Inspection operations are shown as diamonds; everything else as a box.

Flow

Processes against a part

The route the part takes through the works, in order. Operation numbers step in tens so an operation can be inserted later without renumbering everything after it. This routing is what the PFD, the PFMEA and the control plan are all built from — nothing downstream can be generated until it exists.

Routing

Dimensions against an operation

Every characteristic produced or checked at an operation. A dimension marked significant or critical is carried into the PFMEA and the control plan with the tighter treatment those require.
