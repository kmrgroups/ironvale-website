NPD Agent

Takes a part from where it is to a full draft set — routing, dimensions, flow, PFMEA, control plan — one step at a time. It stops at anything it cannot do honestly and tells you why. Everything it writes is a draft. It signs nothing and approves nothing; the AI's proposals are marked as such wherever they appear, so nobody mistakes them for engineering that has been done.

Reads across every module and reports what an auditor would find. The checks themselves are arithmetic, not opinion — the agent runs them, then asks the AI only to put them in the order a person should work through. It changes nothing. Every finding links to the screen where it can be fixed.

Lots

Measurement System Analysis — gauge R&R

The average-and-range study from the AIAG MSA manual. Take the same parts, measure them repeatedly with the same gauge by more than one operator, and the study says how much of what you are seeing is the part and how much is the measurement. A gauge that cannot tell your parts apart makes every inspection record taken with it worth less than it looks.

Gauge register and calibration

Every gauge named on a control plan needs a calibration status. The check below compares the register against the gauges your control plans actually call for — a gauge in use and not on the register is the first thing an auditor finds.

Self Inspection — in process

One sheet per operation per shift. The characteristics and the check frequency come from the control plan. Add a check each time the operator measures; anything out of tolerance has to be answered before the sheet can be closed, including what happened to the parts made since the last good check.

Recent setups

PPAP submission

The eighteen elements, per AIAG. What this system already holds is ticked automatically and shows the document it found — the rest you record as on file, not applicable, or leave outstanding. The warrant cannot be signed while anything required at the chosen level is outstanding.

Elements

Part Submission Warrant

Signing the warrant is a declaration to your customer that the parts meet their requirements and were made at the production rate. It is a statement someone is answerable for.

CNC Programme

Read this before you use anything on this screen. A drafted programme is a starting point for a programmer, not a programme to run. It has not been simulated, the offsets and work coordinates are assumptions, and no machine here knows your fixtures. Post it to a control without proving it out and you will crash a spindle. Every file this screen produces carries that warning inside it, and it cannot be marked proven until someone puts their name to a dry run.

Programme

Prove-out record

Filled in after the programme has been run in single block with feed override down and the first part measured. Until then the programme stays marked unproven wherever it appears.

Control Plan

Built from the PFMEA, the routing and the dimensions, so every control answers a failure mode that was actually ranked. An auditor cross-checks the two documents against each other — generating the control plan from anything else is how they come apart.

Control Plan

Approval

A control plan is what the shop floor is held to. Until it is approved the printed copy is stamped DRAFT.

Process FMEA

Drafted by AI from the routing and the dimensions already on the part — every line is tied to a real operation and a real characteristic. Severity, occurrence and detection are starting points for your CFT to argue with, not answers. Nothing here is approved until a named team signs it.

PFMEA

Cross-functional team sign-off

A PFMEA is a team judgement, not a document one person produces. Record who reviewed it — the printed copy shows this, and until it is filled the print is stamped DRAFT.

Process Flow Diagram

Drawn from the routing — it is not typed separately, so it can never disagree with the operations you entered. Inspection operations are shown as diamonds; everything else as a box.

Flow

Processes against a part

The route the part takes through the works, in order. Operation numbers step in tens so an operation can be inserted later without renumbering everything after it. This routing is what the PFD, the PFMEA and the control plan are all built from — nothing downstream can be generated until it exists.

Routing

Dimensions against an operation

Every characteristic produced or checked at an operation. A dimension marked significant or critical is carried into the PFMEA and the control plan with the tighter treatment those require.
